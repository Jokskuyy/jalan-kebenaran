# Client Solution Case Study — Template

> Portfolio template for public or synthetic work. Never include confidential client data. Label simulations, estimates, and synthetic datasets explicitly.

## Executive summary

In 3–5 sentences, state the user problem, operating constraint, decision, measurable evidence, and honest limitation. Avoid leading with a framework name.

## 1. Problem and users

- Who performs the work?
- What is the as-is workflow and baseline?
- Which evidence supports the problem statement?
- Who owns the operational decision?

## 2. Constraints and non-goals

List safety, privacy, authorization, latency, cost, data, and human-approval boundaries. State what the system deliberately does not do.

## 3. Options and architecture decision

Compare plausible alternatives, explain the chosen architecture, and link the ADR. Include a C4 context/container diagram or another appropriately scoped diagram.

## 4. Delivery flow

Show the end-to-end flow, tool contracts, state transitions, approval points, and terminal states. Explain what happens when evidence is missing.

## 5. Evaluation evidence

Describe the dataset, split, metrics, formulas, results, slices, and error analysis. Every number must link to a reproducible report, notebook, test, or trace.

## 6. Reliability, safety, and operations

Show at least one failure path, detection signal, recovery or rollback, and remaining risk. Include cost and p95 latency where relevant.

## 7. Business interpretation

Translate system metrics into an appropriately qualified operational impact. Clearly distinguish observed evidence from simulated ROI.

## 8. Limitations and next experiment

State what fails today, why it matters, and the smallest next experiment that would reduce the highest-risk uncertainty.
